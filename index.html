About Doctors' SaveALife Foundation

Doctors' Savealife Foundation was founded in 2009 by a team of doctors with a passion to educate our people on preventable death and teaching them what to do where there is no doctor. The Foundation is a medically inclined non-governmental organization with a vision of total health through the power of knowledge and services.

Health safety begins with the ability to identify health dangers and knowing what to do to avert or manage the challenges should such impose at any point in time. We are owners of Save A Life Mission Hospital worldwide. Our affiliate hospitals with robust healthcare facilities have supported us in giving the best medical treatment and care to teeming Nigerians in need of quality healthcare services.

Most importantly, those cases that would have otherwise flown abroad can now be treated and managed at the comfort of our homes. We are currently on our intensive campaign to change the life expectancy index of Nigerians from 55 years to 70 by year 2025. This we hope to achieve through concerted and collaborative efforts in being proactive to people's health through relevant knowledge and action-driven commitments.
